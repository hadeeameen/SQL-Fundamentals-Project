Sales Analytics — Business Insights Report

## Project Overview

This project uses PostgreSQL to analyze sample sales data and answer business questions related to revenue, customer behavior, product performance, and geographic sales.

## Dataset

- 8 customers
- 6 products
- 12 orders
- 15 order-item records

Only completed orders are included in revenue calculations. Cancelled orders are excluded.

## Key Findings

1. **Total revenue:** The sample dataset generates ₹186,500 from 11 completed orders.
2. **Monthly performance:** April generates the highest revenue at ₹60,000, followed by January at ₹56,400.
3. **Category performance:** Electronics generates ₹160,000, approximately 85.8% of total revenue.
4. **Geographic performance:** Kochi generates ₹123,500, approximately 66.2% of total revenue.
5. **Customer retention:** Three of the eight customers have placed at least two completed orders, a repeat-customer rate of 37.5%.

## Recommendations

- Investigate the products and transactions responsible for monthly revenue peaks.
- Monitor Electronics inventory and evaluate opportunities to grow other categories.
- Examine the factors contributing to Kochi's strong sales performance.
- Introduce customer loyalty initiatives to encourage repeat purchases.
- Track these metrics regularly using the monthly sales and customer segment views.

## Limitations

The dataset is small and synthetic. These findings demonstrate SQL analysis techniques and should not be treated as evidence of actual market behavior. The customer segment thresholds are illustrative.

## Tools Used

- PostgreSQL
- SQL joins, aggregates, CTEs, subqueries, CASE expressions, and window functions
- Git and GitHub
